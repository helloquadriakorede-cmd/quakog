Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with a professional book marketer.

About My Book Marketing Services

Who do you work with?

I work with aspiring authors, independent publishers, first-time writers, and established authors looking for book marketing and publishing support across all genres including fiction, self-help, memoirs, children's books, poetry, and more.

Do you only work with published authors?

No. I also welcome authors who are preparing to publish and need assistance with their marketing strategy, branding, or publishing journey before launch.

Can you help promote books already published on Amazon?

Yes. Many authors come to me after publishing because they feel their book is not getting the visibility it deserves. I offer book promotion and marketing support for already-published books, including listing optimization, description rewrites, and promotional campaigns.

Do you help with Amazon KDP?

Yes. I offer KDP related guidance and support with book presentation, descriptions, keywords, categories, and publishing preparation. Amazon is a powerful platform for authors when used correctly.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Absolutely. You can request an individual service like a book description rewrite or a social media campaign, or you can choose one of my packages that combines multiple services for a better value.

How do I get started?

Simply visit my contact page and fill out the enquiry form with information about your book, your goals, and the type of assistance you need. I will review your message and respond with the next steps.

Do you guarantee book sales or bestseller rankings?

No. Book marketing can significantly improve visibility and create opportunities to reach readers, but actual sales and rankings depend on several factors including book quality, genre competition, pricing, and reader preferences. I believe in setting realistic expectations while working hard to maximize your book's exposure.

How much experience do you have?

I have 8 years of experience working as a professional book marketer, helping authors across every genre improve their visibility, strengthen their brands, and connect with readers worldwide.

Where are you located?

I am based in Los Angeles, United States of America. However, I work with authors globally. Distance has never been a barrier to effective book marketing.

What genres do you work with?

I work with all genres. Fiction, non-fiction, self-help, memoirs, children's books, poetry, romance, mystery, Christian literature, academic writing, and more. Every genre has an audience, and my job is to help you find yours.

Still Have Questions?

I am always happy to answer questions from authors who are considering working with a book marketer. Reach out and let's talk about your book.
