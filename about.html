Meet Your Book Marketer

Eight years of experience helping authors across every genre get discovered, get read, and get remembered.

Hello, I'm Quadri

For the past eight years, I have worked as a book marketer, helping authors across different genres take their published work and turn it into something readers actually discover, enjoy, and talk about.

In that time, I have learned one thing clearly. A great book without the right marketing is like a light switched on in an empty room. The brilliance is there, but no one sees it. That is the gap I close for authors.

Over the years, I have worked with authors writing fiction, self-help, memoirs, children's books, poetry, and everything in between. Every genre is different, but the goal remains the same, get the book in front of readers who will genuinely value it.

I approach every project with the same mindset I had on day one. Your book matters. Your story deserves attention. And getting it there takes more than luck, it takes strategy.

My Journey As A Book Marketer

I started working as a book marketer because I saw how many talented authors struggled with visibility. Great books were getting lost simply because no one knew they existed. That is the gap I decided to close.

Over 8 years, I have helped authors across all genres. Fiction writers trying to build a readership. Self-help authors trying to reach people who need their message. Memoir writers trying to share their life stories. Children's book authors trying to connect with parents and young readers. Poets trying to find their audience.

Every project taught me something new. Every author reminded me why this work matters. And today, after hundreds of projects, I am still just as passionate about helping authors succeed as I was when I started.

Based in Los Angeles, United States of America, I work with authors globally. Distance has never been a barrier to good book marketing.
